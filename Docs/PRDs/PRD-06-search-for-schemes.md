# PRD-06: Search for Schemes (Simulated)

---

## 1. Module Overview & Goal
> ⚠️ **MANDATORY ARCHITECTURAL DISCLOSURE (CRITICAL SCOPE BOUNDARY):**  
> The **Search for Schemes** feature is a **SIMULATED / MOCKED** capability. It does **NOT** connect to any live government scheme database, National Scholarship Portal, myscheme.gov.in API, or automated credit sanctioning platform. It must **NEVER** be built, presented, or described in a manner that could mislead a rural entrepreneur into believing that these matches constitute formal statutory eligibility or loan approvals. Every single scheme card generated by this module carries a mandatory, non-removable disclaimer: *"AI-generated illustrative match — verify with your nearest SCA/bank before applying"*.

The goal of this module is to demonstrate how progressive household questioning combined with AI can help a multi-member rural household discover plausible, illustrative central and state concessional credit schemes suited to their specific profile and proposed business. The module takes inputs from a progressive 3-step questionnaire, grounds them against a team-curated catalog of static mock scheme archetypes, and synthesizes structured, categorized recommendation cards using Vertex AI Gemini 2.5 Flash.  
**Traceability:** Sourced from `Docs/01_PRODUCT_VISION.md` (Sections 4, 5: Critical Scope Decision), `Docs/02_USER_PERSONAS.md` (Meena's goals and Explicit Anti-Persona), `Docs/03_USER_JOURNEY_MAPS.md` (Stages 9, 10), `Docs/04_DATA_DICTIONARY_IA.md` (Section 2: `scheme_search_sessions`; Section 4: Mock Scheme Archetype Catalog), and `Docs/05_TECH_STACK_CONSTRAINTS.md` (Section 6).

## 2. In Scope / Out of Scope

### In Scope
- **Simulated Scheme Discovery Engine:** AI-driven generation of plausible, illustrative scheme options using Vertex AI Gemini 2.5 Flash grounded on pre-defined static archetypes.
- **Progressive Household Questionnaire Modal:** A mobile-friendly, multi-step accordion wizard that avoids overwhelming low-literacy users with 30 simultaneous form fields:
  - Step 1: Enterprise Ownership & Primary Operator (*"Who will run this business? Me / My spouse / Joint family / Not decided"*).
  - Step 2: Primary Applicant Profile (Age, Gender, Social Category [SC/ST/OBC/General], Annual Household Income Band, Education, Disability, Ex-Servicemen Status).
  - Step 3: Household Co-Applicant & Subsidy History (Previous government loan/subsidy status, existing registered enterprise, additional family member attributes).
- **Static Mock Scheme Archetype Catalog:** A local, hand-authored JSON catalog of 8–12 representative government credit archetypes (e.g., *Women Entrepreneur Dairy Support*, *Artisan Modernization Concession*, *Differential Rate of Interest Scheme*) to prevent ungrounded, wild hallucinations across demo runs.
- **Structured Categorized Recommendations:** Recommendations grouped into three explicit visual buckets:
  1. *Business-Linked Schemes* (tailored to the chosen sector: Dairy, Kirana, Tailoring, etc.),
  2. *Bank & Lending Institution Schemes* (SCA concessional loan, Regional Rural Bank, SHG linkage),
  3. *Loan-Type & Subsidy Schemes* (Margin money support, interest subvention, capital subsidy).
- **Mandatory UI Disclaimers:** Visible badge on every card: `AI-Generated Illustrative Match` with subtext: *"Verify with your nearest SCA or bank branch before applying."*
- **Database Persistence & PII Protection:** Storing sessions in `scheme_search_sessions` with PII masking (no unencrypted raw PII logged to Cloud Logging).

### Out of Scope
- **Live Government Scheme API Integration:** Absolutely no live calls to `myscheme.gov.in`, `JanSamarth`, `Udyam`, or state portal APIs (per explicit mandate in `01_PRODUCT_VISION.md` Section 5).
- **Statutory Credit Approval / Sanction Letter Issuance:** The app does not issue sanction letters, in-principle sanction certificates, or guarantee bank approval.
- **Automated Scheme Application Submission:** No direct submission of loan applications to bank core systems or government departments.

## 3. User Stories
- **US-6.1 (Stress-Free Household Profiling):** As Meena, I want to answer simple, one-at-a-time questions about my family (like whether my husband or I will own the shop) so that I don't feel intimidated by a massive 30-question government form (`Docs/02_USER_PERSONAS.md`, `Docs/03_USER_JOURNEY_MAPS.md` Stage 9).
- **US-6.2 (Discovering Family Eligibility Advantages):** As Meena, I want to find out if registering the business under my name (as a woman entrepreneur from a backward class) gives my family better interest subsidies than registering under my spouse (`Docs/01_PRODUCT_VISION.md` Section 4, `Docs/Rough Plan/SIH Project draft plan.txt` Line 74).
- **US-6.3 (Honest Illustrative Guidance):** As Meena, I want to clearly understand that these suggested schemes are educational starting points so that I know I must still visit my local bank officer to confirm live availability (`Docs/01_PRODUCT_VISION.md` Section 5, `Docs/02_USER_PERSONAS.md`).
- **US-6.4 (Architectural Integrity for Evaluators):** As an Evaluator inspecting the codebase, I want to see that the simulated nature of this feature is strictly enforced at the data model and API response layer so that the project maintains complete academic and government-grade integrity (`Docs/01_PRODUCT_VISION.md` Section 5, `Docs/05_TECH_STACK_CONSTRAINTS.md` Section 6).

## 4. Functional Requirements

### Progressive Questionnaire Flow
- **FR-6.1:** Clicking "Search for Schemes" on `/report` shall open a multi-step progressive modal dialog.
- **FR-6.2:** The questionnaire shall divide inputs into 3 discrete steps with visual progress indicators:
  - **Step 1 — Enterprise Ownership:**
    - Single-choice radio cards:
      - `[ ] Me (Primary Applicant)`
      - `[ ] My Spouse`
      - `[ ] Joint / Family Enterprise`
      - `[ ] Not Decided Yet`
  - **Step 2 — Primary Applicant Demographics:**
    - Age (Numeric, 18–70),
    - Gender (`Female`, `Male`, `Other`),
    - Social Category (`SC`, `ST`, `OBC`, `General`, `Safai Karamchari`),
    - Household Annual Income Band (`< ₹1.5 Lakh`, `₹1.5L - ₹3.0L`, `₹3.0L - ₹6.0L`, `> ₹6.0L`),
    - Education Level (`No formal education`, `Primary`, `10th Pass`, `12th Pass`, `Graduate`),
    - Disability Status (`Yes`, `No`),
    - Ex-Serviceman Status (`Yes`, `No`).
  - **Step 3 — Household History:**
    - Existing Registered Business (`Yes`, `No`),
    - Previous Government Loan / Subsidy Received (`None`, `MUDRA`, `PMEGP`, `SCA Loan`, `Other`),
    - Optional: Add Co-Applicant Member details (Relationship, Gender, Disability).
- **FR-6.3:** Each step shall validate inputs before enabling the "Next" button. Back navigation shall preserve entered values.

### Mock Grounding & Synthesis Engine
- **FR-6.4:** The backend shall maintain a static, curated archetype catalog (`mock_scheme_archetypes.json`) containing 8–12 scheme patterns, including:
  1. `MICRO_WOMEN_DAIRY`: Women-led dairy micro-units under cooperative refinance.
  2. `NSFDC_MAHILA_SAMRIDDHI`: SC women micro-credit up to ₹1.40L with interest rebate.
  3. `NSKFDC_SWACCHTA_UDYAMI`: Safai Karamchari sanitation and small transport equipment.
  4. `NBCFDC_GENERAL_TERM_LOAN`: OBC manufacturing/retail credit up to ₹50L.
  5. `MUDRA_SHISHU_RETAIL`: Micro retail trading units up to ₹50,000.
  6. `PMEGP_RURAL_ARTISAN`: Rural village industries with margin money capital subsidy.
  7. `NHFDC_DIVYANGJAN_SWAVALAMBAN`: Special concessional loan for persons with benchmark disabilities.
  8. `DRI_SCHEME`: Differential Rate of Interest (4.0%) for ultra-low-income rural families.
- **FR-6.5:** The backend shall construct a grounded prompt to Vertex AI `gemini-2.5-flash`:
  - Injects the user's completed questionnaire JSON,
  - Injects the active assessment's `business_category`, `project_cost`, and `margin_capital`,
  - Injects the archetype catalog,
  - Mandates the model to match and adapt the most relevant 3 to 5 archetypes,
  - Instructs the model to evaluate whether registering under the primary applicant or spouse yields a more favorable concessional credit tier.
- **FR-6.6 (Mandatory Field Enforcement):** Every scheme object in the model's generated JSON array must strictly contain:
  ```json
  {
    "scheme_id": "ARCHETYPE_KEY",
    "scheme_name": "String",
    "category": "business_linked | bank_specific | loan_type_specific",
    "target_beneficiary_match": "String (e.g. Recommended under Wife's name for 1.5% interest subsidy)",
    "illustrative_benefit": "String",
    "indicative_interest_rate": "String",
    "participating_institutions": "String",
    "is_illustrative": true,
    "mandatory_disclosure": "AI-generated illustrative match — verify with your nearest SCA/bank before applying"
  }
  ```

### Rendering & Disclosure Presentation
- **FR-6.7:** The frontend shall render results in 3 categorized tabs or sections:
  - 🏪 **Business-Linked Schemes** (Category-specific concessions),
  - 🏦 **Bank & SCA Channelizing Schemes** (Institution-specific options),
  - 💳 **Loan-Type & Subsidy Schemes** (Subsidies and special tiers).
- **FR-6.8 (UI Disclosure Banner):** A prominent amber notice banner must be fixed above the scheme cards:
  > *"ℹ️ **Demonstration & Advisory Notice:** The schemes shown below are AI-generated illustrative recommendations based on typical government programs. VyapaarSathi does not connect to live scheme databases or guarantee loan approval. Always take this report to your local District Channelizing Agency or bank branch to verify current program availability and eligibility guidelines."*
- **FR-6.9 (Card-Level Badge):** Every individual scheme card must display an indelible badge:
  `⚠️ Illustrative Match` styled in subtle amber/slate.

### Privacy & Persistence
- **FR-6.10:** The session shall be written to `scheme_search_sessions` table linked to the current `assessment_id`.
- **FR-6.11:** Household questionnaire data containing sensitive social categories or income bands shall NOT be logged in application server logs or Cloud Logging plain-text payloads.

## 5. Data Requirements

### Tables & Static Files Read
- **`mock_scheme_archetypes.json` (`Docs/04_DATA_DICTIONARY_IA.md` Section 4):** Curated static catalog of 8–12 reference archetypes.
- **`assessments`:** `assessment_id`, `business_category`, `margin_capital`, `module2_result_json`.

### Tables Written
- **`scheme_search_sessions` Table (`Docs/04_DATA_DICTIONARY_IA.md` Section 2):**
  - `session_id` (BIGINT PK, auto-increment),
  - `assessment_id` (BIGINT FK → assessments),
  - `household_answers_json` (JSON, storing progressive questionnaire responses),
  - `generated_schemes_json` (JSON, array of scheme objects, each with `is_illustrative: true`),
  - `created_at` (TIMESTAMP).
- **`llm_usage_log` Table:**
  - Logs token usage for the Vertex AI scheme synthesis call.

## 6. API / Integration Requirements
- **Vertex AI Generative AI (`gemini-2.5-flash`):** Invoked with system prompt enforcing archetype grounding and the `is_illustrative: true` contract. Temperature set to $0.1$ for consistency.
- **REST Endpoints Exposed:**
  - `POST /api/schemes/search`: Receives `{assessment_id, household_answers}`, queries Vertex AI, writes to `scheme_search_sessions`, and returns the validated illustrative scheme list.
  - `GET /api/schemes/session/{assessment_id}`: Retrieves previously generated illustrative schemes for the report.
- **Fallback Behavior:**
  - If Vertex AI times out or returns an unparseable response, the backend falls back to directly serving 3 pre-matched static archetypes from `mock_scheme_archetypes.json` matching the user's `business_category` and `social_category` with zero hallucination risk.

---

## 7. Edge Cases & Error States
- **EC-6.1 (User Attempts to Apply Directly):** If a user clicks a scheme card expecting an "Apply Now" button, the card instead opens an guidance modal: *"Next Steps: 1. Download your Feasibility PDF. 2. Visit your District Channelizing Agency (SCA) office listed in the Contact tab. 3. Submit your application with Aadhaar and caste certificate."*
- **EC-6.2 (Applicant Already Benefited from Previous Scheme):** If user indicates previous government subsidy received (e.g. PMEGP), the AI engine prioritizes expansion or second-loan schemes (e.g. Term Loan Tier 2) and highlights that first-time capital subsidy may be restricted.
- **EC-6.3 (Incomplete Questionnaire Submission):** The submit button remains disabled until all required fields in Step 1, 2, and 3 are answered, preventing fragmented LLM generation.

## 10. Open Questions / Assumptions
- **Static Archetype Maintenance:** The 8–12 mock archetypes are stored in a version-controlled JSON configuration file within Spring Boot (`src/main/resources/data/mock_scheme_archetypes.json`), requiring no live external maintenance or database migrations.
- **PII Retention Policy:** Sourced from `04_DATA_DICTIONARY_IA.md` Section 2 privacy note, household answers are stored in `scheme_search_sessions` for session reload, but in a production enterprise environment, a data retention purge schedule (e.g. 90-day auto-expiry) would be recommended.
